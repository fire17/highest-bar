## 📝 Description (paste-ready)

I invented a 60-year-old engineer who doesn't exist — a composite CV across the 20 greatest engineering companies in history — and had Claude's frontier model *become* him for a job interview. By the end, "Sol Adler" had written a 34-page Master Engineering book (16 chapters, the Ten Laws, five checklists), 20 per-company doctrine volumes, and a ready-to-load skill that makes cheaper models (Opus, even Sonnet) act from all of it with exact page references.

The wild part: I prepared every message in advance, queued them, set stop-hook goals, and walked away. Zero supervision. The AI even called me on my Echo when the homework was done.

This video is the full trace — every prompt, in order, and why each rung of the ladder works — plus the generalized recipe for any domain, and the meta-skill that now runs the whole play automatically with two agents.

⚙️ The technique in 5 beats: ANCHOR (sparse alternate-reality CV) → INDUCE (the roleplay ladder) → EXTRACT (life story with a hidden schema + corpus assignment) → SYNTHESIZE (the book + self-polish passes) → COMPRESS (the skill with a smart index).
